## English

**App name**
PizzaRush: Pizza Courier

**Short description**
Deliver the pizza hot! A courier adventure through Anatolia and Istanbul.

**Full description**

The pizza is hot, the customer is waiting and the clock is ticking!

In PizzaRush you are a pizza courier. The job sounds simple: get the pizza to the customer. But chasing dogs, cats leaping into the road, panicking chickens, duck families crossing the street, city traffic and crumbling platforms stand in your way. Every bump damages the pizza. Will you go fast, or go careful?

TEN LEVELS FROM ANATOLIA TO ISTANBUL
• The stone streets of Safranbolu
• The fairy chimneys of Cappadocia and a boss fight
• A climb among the stone houses of Mardin
• A rainy, slippery night at Sümela
• Outrunning a street sweeper at Nemrut
• Traffic lights in Maslak
• Tram tracks in Beyoğlu
• The Üsküdar waterfront
• Waiting for the ferry on the Bebek shore
• The grand finale in the Historic Peninsula against the Dough Monster

HOW TO PLAY
• Run, jump, crouch and vault over obstacles
• Clear the way with a melee hit or throw a pizza slice (but every throw costs you pizza!)
• Keep the pizza fresh and beat the clock to earn three stars
• Spend the coins you collect on upgrades and costumes

FEATURES
• 3D characters and levels inspired by real places in Türkiye
• Every customer has their own thank-you and delivery tune
• Achievements, stars and records
• Touch controls designed for phones
• Plays offline

Can you deliver it before it gets cold?
